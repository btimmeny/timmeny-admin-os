## Foundational Discoveries

### Goal progress requires evidence beyond completed tasks

- **Maturity:** Promoted
- **Evidence:** Tennis improvement, marriage time allocation, career progress, fitness goals
- **Underlying problem:** Activity can appear high while meaningful progress remains low or misdirected.
- **Promoted capability:** Goal-progress and Life Health intelligence
- **Related document:** [40 — Product](../40-Product.md)

### Architecture should be discovered through operational use

- **Maturity:** Promoted
- **Evidence:** Daily-life and career workflows repeatedly expose needs that theoretical feature planning would not prioritize correctly.
- **Underlying problem:** Speculative architecture encourages overbuilding and weak product validation.
- **Promoted capability:** Discovery-driven design process
- **Related document:** [20 — Design Process](../20-Design-Process.md)
