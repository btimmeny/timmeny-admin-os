# Timmeny Admin OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Timmeny Admin OS is a personal administrative system operated for its owner. It processes information only to provide requested email-review, task-management, scheduling, and related administrative functions.

## Information processed

The service may process email metadata and content, calendar information, task and project data, user instructions, and operational records required to complete requested actions.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide, secure, audit, and improve the requested Timmeny Admin OS functions. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

## Data sharing

Information may be transmitted to service providers necessary to operate the system, including hosting, database, email, calendar, task-management, and AI service providers. Data is shared only as needed to provide the requested functionality.

## Data retention

Operational records may be retained for continuity, auditability, security, and recovery. Email bodies and other source data are retained only when required by the implemented workflow or connected service.

## Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect access to the service and its credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## User control

The owner may request correction or deletion of retained Timmeny Admin OS data, subject to technical, legal, security, and audit requirements.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through the Timmeny Admin OS project.
