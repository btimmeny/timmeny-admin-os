## Action Approval

- Brian wants the system to recommend an action, show the affected items, accept his approval or correction, and then take the approved action.
- Recommendation approval and external execution must remain separate states.
- Archive and move to Trash require explicit approval during the initial daily-use phase.
- Permanent deletion is not an initial capability.
- Replies should be drafted before sending, and sending requires explicit approval of the content.
- Bulk approval is useful for homogeneous high-confidence recommendations, but each item must retain its own decision and execution record.
- Failed actions should remain visible and retryable rather than disappearing from the review.
