# Brian Operating Preferences

## Status

These preferences were learned through real inbox-review discussions. They remain subject to refinement through daily use.

## Inbox Review

- Present one ordered table rather than separate disconnected summaries.
- Put items requiring Brian's action first.
- Every email or thread must have a proposed next action, even when the recommendation is to wait, remind, archive, or move it to Trash.
- During the bootstrap phase, expose rich metadata so Brian can correct outcomes, actions, entities, relationships, dependencies, and dispositions.
- Explicitly ask for feedback about groupings, entities, relationships, dependencies, and missing context.

## Source Grounding

- Refresh Gmail when Brian requests an inbox or operational review; do not rely on remembered inbox contents.
- Read the current Git operating state before making conclusions about current curated operational state.
- Treat screenshots as current evidence for systems without live integrations.

## Executive Advisory Pipeline

Group expert-network and consulting opportunities under an Executive Advisory Pipeline while retaining each opportunity as a distinct item.

For each opportunity assess separately:

- Capability fit: whether Brian can credibly advise based on his experience.
- Strategic fit: whether the topic aligns with where Brian wants to spend time and reinforce his current positioning.

Current strategic preference favors opportunities involving:

- enterprise AI
- agentic engineering and AI-native software delivery
- engineering transformation and developer productivity
- enterprise architecture
- CTO / CIO leadership and enterprise transformation
- financial-services or retail technology transformation when strategically relevant

Strong historical expertise that may have lower current strategic interest includes retail operations, RFID, supply chain, point of sale, omnichannel commerce, cloud, and DevOps. These may be high capability fit but should not automatically receive an engage recommendation.

Specific learned example:

- RFID is a strong capability fit because of Brian's Walmart background, but generally a lower strategic-interest opportunity than AI-focused advisory work.

2. Sherry's insurance check arrives at Brian's mother.
3. The escrow insurance check arrives at Brian's mother.

Known reading sources include:

Brian has also explicitly approved moving previously reviewed reading-queue items to Trash. Do not generalize permanent deletion to all future reading material without reviewing the current item or an established sender rule.

## Mass Mailings

Group volunteer solicitations, promotions, retail offers, and similar broad distributions as Mass Mailings during review.

Default proposed disposition is archive or move to Trash, subject to Brian's confirmation and learned sender rules.

Repeated confirmed removal may support a future unsubscribe recommendation.
